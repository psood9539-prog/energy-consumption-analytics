Energy Consumption Analytics

About the Project

Why this dashboard was built, what it answers and the tools behind it.

Problem statement

The problem

Electricity providers record thousands of meter readings, but raw rows of units and bills do not show where demand is concentrated, which consumer groups drive billing, or how much of the load falls in peak hours. Without a clear summary it is hard to plan supply and understand billing. This project turns 60,000 readings (January 2024 – December 2025) into one dashboard that answers these questions quickly.

Objectives

What the project aims to do

  • Show total units consumed and billing as clear KPIs.
  • Track the monthly consumption trend across 2024 and 2025.
  • Compare regions and consumer types (Residential, Commercial, Industrial).
  • Measure how much use falls in peak hours compared with off-peak hours.
  • Explain how billing is spread across the three tariff slabs.
  • Identify the top 10 consumers by units and share findings that the data supports.
